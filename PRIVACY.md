# Paste enabler Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Paste enabler is a browser extension developed by JonLuca DeCaro. It restores common browser interactions on sites that try to disable pasting, copying, text selection, context menus, or standard input behavior.

## Data handling

The extension runs locally on webpages to remove restrictive event handlers and element attributes. It does not read, record, store, or transmit clipboard contents, keystrokes, passwords, form-field values, selected text, submitted data, page content, or browsing history.

## Settings storage

Paste enabler stores one local boolean setting indicating whether the extension is enabled or disabled. That setting stays in browser-managed extension storage on the user's device and is not sent to the developer.

## Collection and sharing

The extension makes no external network requests of its own and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It uses no analytics or advertising services.

## Retention and user control

The enabled/disabled setting remains until the user changes it, clears extension data, or removes the extension.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document and its updated date. Questions may be sent to [chrome@jonlu.ca](mailto:chrome@jonlu.ca).
